About This Game

Run a small enchantment workshop as a non-mage who designs magical diagrams for customers who can power them with mana.

Controls

  • Left MouseDraw diagrams; click a guide rune, then click the slate to place a tracing guide
  • Right MouseHold on the slate to erase ink; right-click a selected guide rune to deselect
  • T / DTest the diagram / deliver the product
  • R / NResearch new runes / decline the current commission
  • S / LSave / load the ledger
